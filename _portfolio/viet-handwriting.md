Undergraduate thesis project addressing the unique challenges of Vietnamese handwritten text recognition: a 29-letter alphabet augmented with 5 tone marks and 7 diacritics, totalling 134 distinct character classes.

The work proposes an end-to-end deep neural network combining a CNN backbone with a Transformer-based sequence decoder, achieving results competitive with contemporary state-of-the-art approaches on the VNOnDB benchmark.

**Published at:** 2020 7th NAFOSTED Conference on Information and Computer Science  
**Award:** Best Paper (2nd author)

## Key Contributions

- Adapted Transformer architecture for the Vietnamese character set (134 classes including diacritics and tone marks)
- Competitive word accuracy against prior RNN/LSTM-based methods on VNOnDB
